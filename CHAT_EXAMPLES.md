# 💬 Natural Language Chat Examples

## For Non-Technical Users

### Just Say What You Want!

#### Data Cleaning
- "clean my data"
- "preprocess this dataset"
- "fix missing values"
- "prepare for analysis"
- "clean everything automatically"

#### Visualization
- "show me plots"
- "visualize this"
- "create charts"
- "make some graphs"
- "plot everything"

#### Analysis
- "analyze this dataset"
- "what's in this data?"
- "summarize this file"
- "tell me about my data"
- "what do I have here?"

#### Questions
- "what should I do next?"
- "how do I clean this?"
- "what's wrong with my data?"
- "recommend preprocessing steps"
- "explain the columns"

## The Agent Understands:

### Generic Terms ✅
- "appropriately" → Agent decides best method
- "everything" → Agent processes all applicable operations
- "fix" → Agent identifies and resolves issues
- "prepare" → Agent does full ML-ready preprocessing

### Context ✅
- Inspects your data characteristics
- Chooses optimal techniques automatically
- Explains what it did and why
- Saves results for download

### No Keywords Needed! ✅
You DON'T need to say:
- ❌ "use median imputation"
- ❌ "apply one-hot encoding"
- ❌ "standard scaling"
- ❌ "drop high-missing columns"

Just say:
- ✅ "clean my data"
And the agent figures out the rest!
